Nukkad Natak

Nukkad Natak (translated as "Street Play") is a highly energetic, raw, and minimalist form of Indian street theatre performed in outdoor public spaces to educate the masses and spark social change. Originating as a grassroots tool for political activism and community messaging, it strips away the traditional boundaries of formal theatre by removing ticket fees, physical stages, and elaborate backdrops. Instead, performers form a human circle directly in markets, street corners, or parks to captivate everyday passersby.
